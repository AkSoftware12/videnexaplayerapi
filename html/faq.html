General Questions

Is Vidnexa Player completely free?

+

Yes, Vidnexa Player is 100% free. All 8 features — 4K video player, WhatsApp Status Saver, Reel Downloader, Music Player, Gallery, and Private Vault — are completely free with no hidden charges or subscriptions.

Does Vidnexa Player work without internet (offline)?

+

Yes! Vidnexa Player works fully offline. You can play local videos in 4K HDR, listen to local music, browse your gallery, and access the Private Vault — all without any internet connection.

Is Vidnexa available on iPhone (iOS)?

+

Vidnexa Player is primarily available for Android. An iOS version is currently in development and will be available on the App Store soon. Follow us on social media for updates.

What Android version is required?

+

Vidnexa Player requires Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or higher. It works on virtually all Android phones from 2015 onwards, including low-end and budget devices.

Video Playback

What video formats does Vidnexa support?

+

Vidnexa supports 200+ formats: MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, FLV, WMV, HEVC/H.265, VP9, AV1, 3GP, M4V, WebM, and all subtitle formats (SRT, SSA, ASS, VTT).

Does Vidnexa support 4K and HDR?

+

Yes! Vidnexa Player uses hardware GPU decoding to smoothly play 4K Ultra HD and HDR10 content. It also includes SDR+ color enhancement for non-HDR videos.

What is SDR+ in Vidnexa?

+

SDR+ is Vidnexa's proprietary color enhancement technology. It intelligently boosts color vibrancy, contrast and brightness on standard SDR videos, making them look vivid and rich — even on non-HDR screens.

Does Vidnexa support subtitles?

+

Yes, Vidnexa supports all major subtitle formats: SRT, SSA, ASS, VTT, and embedded subtitles in MKV/MP4 files. You can also adjust subtitle size, color, and position.

Downloading

How to save WhatsApp Status with Vidnexa?

+

Open Vidnexa Player → go to Status Saver tab. It automatically shows all viewable WhatsApp statuses. Tap the download icon next to any photo or video status to save it instantly.

How to download Instagram Reels for free?

+

Copy the Instagram Reel link → Open Vidnexa → Downloader tab → Paste link → Tap Download. Saved in HD with zero watermark, completely free.

Is there any watermark on downloaded videos?

+

No. Videos downloaded using Vidnexa's Reel Downloader are saved in original HD quality with absolutely no watermark added.

Which platforms can I download from?

+

Currently Vidnexa supports Instagram Reels and Facebook videos. YouTube support is coming soon. More platforms are being added based on user feedback.

Privacy & Security

Is the Private Vault really secure?

+

Yes. Private Vault uses AES encryption. It supports PIN, pattern, and fingerprint authentication. Files are completely hidden from other apps and the main gallery.

Does Vidnexa collect my personal data?

+

No. Vidnexa Player does not collect or upload your personal files or data. Your media files stay on your device. See our full Privacy Policy for details.
