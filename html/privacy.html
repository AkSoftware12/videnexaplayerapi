Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Vidnexa Player ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Vidnexa Player mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Contact information when you reach out to us (name, email address)
  • Feedback and support requests you voluntarily submit

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device type and operating system version (for crash reporting and compatibility)
  • App version in use
  • Anonymous usage analytics (feature usage patterns, not personal data)
  • Crash logs and error reports

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your personal media files (videos, photos, music)
  • Contents of your Private Vault
  • WhatsApp messages or contacts
  • Downloaded video content
  • Your precise location
  • Any personally identifiable information beyond what you voluntarily provide

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Improve app performance, stability and features
  • Respond to your support requests and feedback
  • Fix bugs and crashes reported through error logs
  • Understand which features are most used (in aggregate, anonymous form)
  • Send important app update notices (only if you opt in)

3. Data Storage and Security

Your media files, Private Vault contents, and downloaded videos are stored entirely on your device. We do not upload, access, or store your personal media files on our servers.

The Private Vault uses AES-256 encryption. Vault contents are encrypted at rest on your device and are never transmitted to any server.

Anonymous analytics data is transmitted securely using HTTPS encryption.

4. Third-Party Services

Vidnexa Player may use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — for crash reporting (Google's privacy policy applies)
  • Google Play Services — for app distribution and updates

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not sell your data to any third party, ever.

5. Permissions Explained

  • Storage/Media: Required to read and play your local video and music files
  • Internet: Required for the Reel Downloader and online music features (optional features)
  • Notification: For download completion alerts (you can disable this)
  • Biometric/Fingerprint: For Private Vault fingerprint unlock (data stays on device)

6. Children's Privacy

Vidnexa Player is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics data collection (in app Settings)
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through an in-app notice or by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@vidnexaplayer.com
  • Website: Contact Form
