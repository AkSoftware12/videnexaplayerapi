Ultra HD Video Player

Smooth 4K & 8K playback with gesture controls for brightness, volume and skip.

Plays 4K and 8K HDR video without stutter or cropping, across 200+ formats including MKV, HEVC and AVI — no separate codec pack needed. Gesture controls handle brightness, volume, seek and playback speed with a swipe, and subtitles load automatically in SRT, SSA, ASS or VTT.

Music Player

Background play, playlists sorted by artist, album or folder.

Play audio in the background while browsing or using other apps, with playlists sorted automatically by artist, album or folder. A built-in equaliser lets you shape the sound to your headphones or speakers.

Stream via Link

Paste any video URL or IPTV stream link and it starts playing instantly — no downloading, no separate streaming app.

File Manager

Browse & organise storage.

Photo Gallery

Every photo on your device, viewable offline in any format.

Every photo on your device in one place, viewable offline in any format — with built-in filters, auto-collage, a duplicate and junk cleaner, and 4K photo enhancement.

Night Mode

A dimmed, warm-toned playback screen that's easier on the eyes for late-night viewing, without touching the actual video quality.

Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Is Vidnexa Player free?

Yes. Vidnexa Player is completely free on the Google Play Store, with no data collected.

Does Vidnexa support 4K and 8K HDR video?

Yes. Vidnexa plays 4K and 8K video with HDR+ tone mapping for brighter highlights, deeper contrast and more accurate colour on supported Android devices.

What is AI voice control in Vidnexa Player?

AI voice control lets you play, pause, skip, adjust volume and search your library using spoken commands, so you can control playback completely hands-free.

Which video formats does Vidnexa support?

Vidnexa supports 200+ formats including MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, FLV, WEBM, 3GP, WMV, HEVC and RMVB, plus audio formats MP3, AAC, OGG, FLAC, WAV and M4A.

Can Vidnexa stream video from a URL?

Yes. Paste any supported video link into Vidnexa and it starts streaming instantly, with no download required.
