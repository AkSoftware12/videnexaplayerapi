Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Is Vidnexa Player free?+

Yes. Vidnexa Player is free to download on the Google Play Store (contains ads · in-app purchases) and needs no sign-up or account.

Does Vidnexa support 4K and 8K HDR video?+

Yes. Vidnexa plays 4K and 8K video on supported Android devices, and its HDR+ mode enhances the picture with brighter highlights, deeper contrast and richer colour.

What is AI voice control in Vidnexa Player?+

AI voice control lets you play, pause, skip, adjust volume and search your library using spoken commands, so you can control playback completely hands-free.

Which video formats does Vidnexa support?+

Vidnexa supports 200+ formats including MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, FLV, WEBM, 3GP, WMV, HEVC and RMVB, plus audio formats MP3, AAC, OGG, FLAC, WAV and M4A.

What is the Network Player in Vidnexa Player?+

Network Player plays media from direct links that you add yourself, such as files on your own server or sources you are authorized to access. There are no pre-loaded links, playlists or channels in the app.

Can I save the audio from a video in Vidnexa Player?+

Yes. While a video is playing, open Player Controls and tap To audio. Save the sound of the full video, or just an A-B part, as an M4A (AAC) audio file that plays on almost any device. You can also cut a short clip or GIF from the same menu.
