FAQ

Common Questions.

Is Vidnexa Player free to download?

+

Yes, Vidnexa Ultra HD Video Player is completely free to download and use on Android. Available on Google Play with no subscription or in-app purchases required for core features.

What video formats does Vidnexa support?

+

Video: MP4, MKV, AVI, FLV, MOV, 3GP, WMV, WEBM. Audio: MP3, AAC, OGG, FLAC, WAV, M4A. No extra codec packs needed — Vidnexa handles everything automatically.

Can Vidnexa play 4K videos?

+

Yes, Vidnexa supports 4K Ultra HD and 1080p Full HD video playback with smooth performance and easy gesture controls for brightness, volume and forward/rewind.

Can I stream online videos without downloading?

+

Yes! Vidnexa has a built-in online video player. Simply paste any video URL to stream it instantly — no download required. Supports most streaming video links.

Does Vidnexa collect any personal data?

+

No. According to the Google Play Store data safety declaration, Vidnexa does not collect any data and shares no data with third parties. Your media files stay on your device.

What Android version is required?

+

Vidnexa Player works on Android 5.0 (Lollipop) and above. It is compatible with virtually all Android phones released after 2015, including low-end and budget devices.
