Everything Vidnexa Player can do.

01 · Video player

Plays the files your phone won't.

Vidnexa plays 4K and 8K video in 200+ formats — MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, FLV, WEBM, HEVC and more — with built-in decoding, so you don't need codec packs.

4K & 8K playback

High-resolution files with hardware decoding where your phone supports it, and a software decoder as a fallback.

HDR+ picture modes

Presets such as HDR+, Warm, Sepia, Neon City, Blue, Green and Dark boost contrast and colour while you watch.

Subtitles

SRT, SSA, ASS and VTT, embedded or external, with subtitle sync if the timing is off.

Audio tracks & sync

Switch between audio languages in dual-audio files and fix audio that runs ahead or behind.

Gestures

Swipe the left side for brightness, the right side for volume, and double-tap to skip.

Floating window & background play

Keep watching in a small window over other apps, or let the audio carry on in the background.

How to use it

  1. Open the Home tab — Vidnexa finds the videos on your phone and groups them by folder.
  2. Tap a video to play it. Turn the phone sideways for full screen.
  3. If a file shows a black screen or stutters, open Player Controls and try the other option under Decoder.

New and handy tools

To audio

Save the sound of a whole video, or just an A-B part, as an M4A (AAC) file in Music / Vidnexa.

Clip / GIF

Cut a favourite moment into a short clip or a GIF to keep or share.

Bookmarks

Mark a moment and jump straight back to it later.

Media info

See a file's details, such as its codec and resolution.

Seek time

Choose how far double-tap and the ± buttons jump: 5, 10, 15 or 30 seconds.

Hold for 2× speed

Long-press the video to watch at double speed; let go to return to normal.

How to use it

  1. Start a video.
  2. Open Player Controls from the player.
  3. Tap a tool. For a part of a video, set points A and B with A-B repeat first, then use To audio.

03 · Music player

A full music player, built in.

The Music tab turns the audio on your phone into a proper library, with themes for the player and a real equalizer.

Your library

Songs, Artists, Albums and Folders, plus shortcuts for Favourites, Most played, Recently added and audio from chats.

Shuffle all

Start your whole collection in random order with one tap.

Player themes

Change the look of the now-playing screen — Candy, Ocean, Midnight, Sunset, Forest or Gold.

Queue

See what plays next, reorder or remove songs, shuffle, clear, and turn autoplay on or off.

Equalizer

5 or 10 bands with presets such as Normal, Movie, Dialogue and Night — and save your own as a profile.

Timer, speed & lyrics

Sleep timer, playback speed, lyrics and volume right on the player.

How to use it

  1. Open the Music tab.
  2. Tap Shuffle all songs, or pick a song, artist, album or folder.
  3. On the player, tap EQ to shape the sound, or the palette icon to change the theme.

04 · Gallery & photo tools

Your photos, sorted and cleaned up.

The Gallery tab has three parts: Photos, Albums and Tools.

Photos by day

Your photos and videos grouped by date, with the length shown on every video.

Album slideshow

Play any album as a slideshow in one tap.

Albums your way

Pinned albums at the top; sort the rest by Recent, A–Z or Most items.

Smart search

Find photos by date, folder, size or colour after a quick photo index.

Duplicate finder & junk cleaner

Group similar shots, find blurry photos and old screenshots, and free up space.

Enhance & collage

Enhance photos up to 4× and build an auto collage that fits your shapes.

How to use it

  1. Open the Gallery tab.
  2. In Tools, tap Scan now once so the photo index can power smart search and cleaning.
  3. Open an album and tap Slideshow, or pick a tool such as Junk cleaner.

05 · Status Saver

Keep a status before it disappears.

Statuses vanish after 24 hours. The built-in Status Saver shows the statuses you've already viewed and lets you keep the ones you like.

Viewed statuses

Photos and videos you have already opened in your messaging app appear in Status Saver.

Full-screen preview

Browse them full-screen before you decide.

Save in one tap

Download to your gallery in original quality, with no watermark.

Share

Send a saved file anywhere you like.

How to use it

  1. Open the status in your messaging app first, so your phone stores it.
  2. Open Status Saver in Vidnexa.
  3. Tap download on the photo or video you want to keep.

Please respect other people's privacy: only save and share content the owner would be comfortable with.

06 · Screen Cast

Watch it on the big screen.

Vidnexa uses Google Cast. Send videos, photos and music to a Chromecast, a Google TV device, or any TV with Chromecast built in on the same Wi-Fi.

Your phone is the remote

Pause, seek or lock the screen while the TV plays.

Original file

Casting sends the file itself, so quality depends on your TV and Wi-Fi, not your phone screen.

No Chromecast?

Most Samsung and LG TVs don't support Google Cast. Use your phone's screen mirroring instead — our guide shows how.

How to use it

  1. Connect the phone and TV to the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Play something in Vidnexa and tap the cast icon.
  3. Choose your TV from the list.

08 · AI voice control

Just say it.

Control playback hands-free: play, pause, skip, change the volume or search your library with your voice.

Hands-free playback

Useful when you're cooking, exercising or the phone is out of reach.

Optional

09 · Network Player

Play from a link.

Play media straight from a direct link you add yourself — for example a file on your own server, or a source you're authorised to use. There are no pre-loaded links, playlists or channels in the app.

Stream without downloading

The file plays while it loads.

Your links only

Vidnexa doesn't provide content; it plays the links you add.

How to use it

  1. Open Network Player.
  2. Paste a direct media link and tap play.

Good to know

What HDR+ is — and what it isn't.

HDR video formats

HDR10, HDR10+, HLG and Dolby Vision are stored inside the video file. To see the real effect you need an HDR-capable screen.

Vidnexa HDR+

A picture-enhancement mode you switch on while watching. Presets like HDR+, Warm and Neon City lift contrast, brightness and colour — handy for dull or dark videos. It doesn't turn a file into true HDR.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Vidnexa Player free?

Yes. It is free to download from Google Play. The free version contains ads, and there are optional in-app purchases. No sign-up or account is needed.

Can Vidnexa save the audio from a video?

Yes. While a video is playing, open Player Controls and tap To audio. You can save the sound of the whole video, or just an A-B part, as an M4A (AAC) file.

Is HDR+ the same as HDR10?

No. HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision are formats inside a video file. Vidnexa's HDR+ is a picture-enhancement mode with presets that boost contrast, brightness and colour while you watch, and it works on ordinary videos too.

Which TVs can Vidnexa cast to?

Chromecast devices, Google TV devices and TVs with Chromecast built in, such as most Android TV and Google TV models. For TVs without Google Cast, including most Samsung and LG models, use your phone's screen mirroring.

Which subtitle formats are supported?

SRT, SSA, ASS and VTT, embedded in the video or as a separate file, with subtitle sync.
