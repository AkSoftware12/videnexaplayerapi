Vidnexa — Ultra HD Video Player

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Vidnexa app handles, what leaves your device, and what stays on it. Vidnexa does not require an account, and we do not run servers that store your personal files.

The short version. Your videos, music, photos and files stay on your phone. Vidnexa never uploads them to our servers or the internet. When you cast, the file you choose is sent over your own Wi-Fi to the TV you pick. The information that leaves your device is advertising, analytics and diagnostic data used by Google AdMob and Firebase, and purchase status handled by Google Play — all described below.

Who we are

Vidnexa — Ultra HD Video Player ("Vidnexa", "the app", "we", "us") is an Android application published on Google Play under the package name com.vidnexa.videoplayer. The app is operated from Uttar Pradesh, India.

Your media stays on your device

Vidnexa is an offline media player. To show and play your files, the app reads video, audio and image files stored on your device and SD card, and the built-in file manager lets you browse, rename, move and delete them.

All of this happens locally. We do not upload, copy, transmit or store your media files, file names, folder structure or thumbnails on any server. We have no access to them and no ability to view them.

If you use the Network Player, the link you add is used to request the media directly from the server that hosts it, so it can be streamed. We do not receive or store the links you play on any server of ours.

Files you create with the player tools — such as screenshots, clips, GIFs, audio saved from a video (To audio), bookmarks and equalizer settings — are created and saved on your device only. Files leave your device only if you choose to share them yourself through Android's share menu.

Status Saver

The Status Saver shows status photos and videos that are already stored on your phone because you viewed them in your messaging app. To do this, the app asks for access to that folder through Android's file access prompt. Vidnexa reads these files locally and, when you tap download, saves a copy to your gallery. Nothing is uploaded or sent to us. Please respect other people's privacy and only save and share content you have permission to use.

Screen Cast

Screen Cast uses Google Cast. To find TVs and Cast devices, the app looks for devices on the Wi-Fi network your phone is connected to. When you cast, the selected video, photo or song is streamed from your phone to that device over your local network. Vidnexa does not send this media to our servers. Google Cast devices are operated under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Information collected by advertising and analytics

Vidnexa is free and supported by advertising. To display ads and understand how the app performs, we use Google services that collect a limited amount of information from your device.

Google AdMob

Ads inside Vidnexa are served by Google AdMob. AdMob collects and shares the following for the purpose of advertising and marketing:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable identifier assigned to your device by Google, used to select and measure ads.
  • Device and other identifiers — device model, operating system version, and similar technical details.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address, so ads are relevant to your general region. Vidnexa does not request precise GPS location.
  • Ad interaction data — whether an ad was shown, viewed or tapped.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's policy on how it uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

We use Google Firebase to understand basic usage patterns and to find crashes. This collects app interactions (such as which screens are opened), crash logs and diagnostic information about app performance. This data is used to keep the app working and to improve it. It is not used to identify you personally.

Push notifications

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send occasional notifications about updates and features. To deliver these, Firebase assigns your installation a messaging token — an identifier tied to the app on your device, not to you personally. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings, and the app will keep working normally.

Voice control

Vidnexa includes an optional voice control feature for commands such as play, pause, skip, volume and search. When you tap the microphone button, your speech is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition service, which on most Android devices is provided by Google. Depending on your device settings, that audio may be sent to Google's servers to be converted into text.

The microphone is only active while voice control is listening. We do not record, store or transmit audio ourselves, and no recordings are kept after the command text is produced. If you never use the voice button, no audio is ever captured. You can deny or revoke the microphone permission in your device settings without affecting the rest of the app.

Private vault and app lock

If you use the private folder or app lock, your PIN is hashed and stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot recover it for you. Biometric unlock is handled entirely by Android — the app receives only a yes or no result and never sees your fingerprint or face data.

Premium and in-app purchases

Vidnexa offers optional in-app purchases, such as Premium, through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play — we never see or store your card or payment details. The app receives only the purchase status from Google Play so it can unlock what you bought, and it may store that status on your device. Your purchase history is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Ratings, update checks and in-app messages

  • Rating prompt: the app may show Google Play's in-app review dialog. Your rating and review are handled by Google Play and shown publicly there; we only see what Google Play publishes.
  • Update check: the app may check Google Play to see whether a newer version is available. This sends the app's package name to Google Play, not your personal information.
  • In-app messages: we may use Firebase In-App Messaging to show occasional messages about features inside the app. This uses a Firebase installation identifier, similar to the messaging token described above.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Media and storage access (photos, videos, audio, or only the photos you select) — to find and play the files on your device, power the gallery and Status Saver, and let the file manager work.
  • Internet and network state — to load ads, stream media in the Network Player, find Cast devices and check for updates.
  • Microphone — optional, and only used while voice control is listening.
  • Notifications — optional, to show playback controls and occasional app updates.
  • Background playback (foreground service) and keep awake — so music and video audio can keep playing with controls in the notification, and the screen doesn't turn off while you watch.
  • Audio settings — to apply the equalizer, volume boost and other sound effects.
  • Biometrics — optional, to unlock your private folder.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob as described above.

You can review or revoke permissions at any time in your device's Settings. Revoking media access will stop the app from listing your files.

Your choices

Reset or delete your Advertising ID

On most Android devices, open Settings → Google → All services → Ads. From there you can reset your Advertising ID or delete it entirely. Deleting it stops apps from receiving a personalised identifier.

Turn off personalised ads

In the same Ads settings screen you can opt out of ad personalisation. You will still see ads in Vidnexa, but they will not be based on your interests.

Consent in Europe and the UK

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form before serving personalised ads, and you can change your choice later from within the app.

US state privacy laws

If you live in a US state with its own privacy law (such as California), the app shows a privacy message through Google's consent tools where required. You can use it to opt out of the sale or sharing of your information for personalised advertising, and change your choice later from the app's privacy options.

How long data is kept

We do not hold a database of users. The advertising and diagnostic data described above is retained by Google according to its own retention schedules. Since we do not collect personal information or operate accounts, there is no personal data held by us to delete.

Children

Vidnexa is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will act on it.

Security

Data transmitted by the app is sent over encrypted connections. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we use accepted practices to protect information in transit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Updates are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continuing to use Vidnexa after a change means you accept the revised policy.
