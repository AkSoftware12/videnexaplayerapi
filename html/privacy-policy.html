Vidnexa — Ultra HD Video Player

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

This policy explains what information the Vidnexa app handles, what leaves your device, and what stays on it. Vidnexa does not require an account, and we do not run servers that store your personal files.

The short version. Your videos, music, photos and files stay on your phone. Vidnexa never uploads them anywhere. The only information that leaves your device is advertising and diagnostic data used by Google AdMob and Firebase, described in full below.

Who we are

Vidnexa — Ultra HD Video Player ("Vidnexa", "the app", "we", "us") is an Android application published on Google Play under the package name com.vidnexa.videoplayer. The app is operated from Uttar Pradesh, India.

Your media stays on your device

Vidnexa is an offline media player. To show and play your files, the app reads video, audio and image files stored on your device and SD card, and the built-in file manager lets you browse, rename, move and delete them.

All of this happens locally. We do not upload, copy, transmit or store your media files, file names, folder structure or thumbnails on any server. We have no access to them and no ability to view them.

If you use the "play via link" feature, the video URL you paste is sent to the server hosting that video so it can be streamed. We do not record or store the links you play.

Information collected by advertising and analytics

Vidnexa is free and supported by advertising. To display ads and understand how the app performs, we use Google services that collect a limited amount of information from your device.

Google AdMob

Ads inside Vidnexa are served by Google AdMob. AdMob collects and shares the following for the purpose of advertising and marketing:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable identifier assigned to your device by Google, used to select and measure ads.
  • Device and other identifiers — device model, operating system version, and similar technical details.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address, so ads are relevant to your general region. Vidnexa does not request precise GPS location.
  • Ad interaction data — whether an ad was shown, viewed or tapped.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's policy on how it uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

We use Google Firebase to understand basic usage patterns and to find crashes. This collects app interactions (such as which screens are opened), crash logs and diagnostic information about app performance. This data is used to keep the app working and to improve it. It is not used to identify you personally.

Push notifications

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send occasional notifications about updates and features. To deliver these, Firebase assigns your installation a messaging token — an identifier tied to the app on your device, not to you personally. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings, and the app will keep working normally.

Voice search

Vidnexa includes an optional voice command feature for finding videos. When you tap the microphone button, your speech is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition service, which on most Android devices is provided by Google. Depending on your device settings, that audio may be sent to Google's servers to be converted into text.

The microphone is only active while you are holding or have started the voice search. We do not record, store or transmit audio ourselves, and no recordings are kept after the search text is produced. If you never use the voice button, no audio is ever captured. You can deny or revoke the microphone permission in your device settings without affecting the rest of the app.

Private vault and app lock

If you use the private folder or app lock, your PIN is hashed and stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot recover it for you. Biometric unlock is handled entirely by Android — the app receives only a yes or no result and never sees your fingerprint or face data.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Media and storage access — to find and play the video, audio and image files on your device, and to let the file manager work.
  • Internet access — to load ads and to stream video when you paste a link.
  • Microphone — optional, and only used while voice search is listening.
  • Notifications — optional, to show playback controls and occasional app updates.
  • Biometrics — optional, to unlock your private folder.

You can review or revoke permissions at any time in your device's Settings. Revoking media access will stop the app from listing your files.

Your choices

Reset or delete your Advertising ID

On most Android devices, open Settings → Google → All services → Ads. From there you can reset your Advertising ID or delete it entirely. Deleting it stops apps from receiving a personalised identifier.

Turn off personalised ads

In the same Ads settings screen you can opt out of ad personalisation. You will still see ads in Vidnexa, but they will not be based on your interests.

Consent in Europe and the UK

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form before serving personalised ads, and you can change your choice later from within the app.

How long data is kept

We do not hold a database of users. The advertising and diagnostic data described above is retained by Google according to its own retention schedules. Since we do not collect personal information or operate accounts, there is no personal data held by us to delete.

Children

Vidnexa is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will act on it.

Security

Data transmitted by the app is sent over encrypted connections. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we use accepted practices to protect information in transit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Updates are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continuing to use Vidnexa after a change means you accept the revised policy.
