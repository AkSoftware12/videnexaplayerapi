7 MX Player Alternatives for Android in 2026

By Vidnexa Team·6 min read·Updated November 2026
Quick answer: For a free, open-source player, try VLC, mpv, Nova Video Player, Just Player or Next Player. For a big home-media library, try Kodi. For one app that combines video with music, gallery, Status Saver and casting, try Vidnexa Player.

MX Player has been a favourite for years, but its free version shows ads and some features sit behind the paid tier. If you're looking for something different, these are the alternatives worth trying.

1. VLC: the all-rounder

Free and open source, VLC plays almost any format and handles network streams well. Its interface is functional rather than flashy. Best for: people who want one reliable player.

2. Vidnexa Player: video plus everyday media tools

Plays 200+ formats including MKV and HEVC, with subtitles (SRT, SSA, ASS, VTT), audio-track switching, HDR+ picture presets and Google Cast. Its Player Controls include an HW decoder switch, audio and subtitle sync, A-B repeat, sleep timer, PIP, bookmarks and Clip / GIF. It adds a music player with equalizer, gallery tools, a WhatsApp Status Saver and voice control. Free to download and supported by ads. Best for: people who'd rather have one app than several.

3. mpv-android: for enthusiasts

An open-source Android version of the mpv player, known for high-quality rendering and deep configuration. It's minimal and takes some setup. Best for: people who like to fine-tune playback.

4. Nova Video Player: library-focused

An open-source player that organises movies and series with artwork and information, and can play from network shares. Best for: building a tidy local library.

5. Just Player: simple and open source

A lightweight, open-source player built on Android's ExoPlayer, with a clean interface and good format support. Best for: a no-frills player.

6. Next Player: modern and lightweight

An open-source player with a modern Material design, gestures and subtitle support. Best for: a clean, simple everyday player.

7. Kodi: the media centre

Kodi is a full open-source media centre for large libraries, add-ons and TV boxes. It's more than most people need on a phone, but powerful if you run a home server. Best for: big libraries and Android TV boxes.

How to choose

  • No ads matters most: VLC, mpv, Nova, Just Player or Next Player.
  • Lots of features in one app: Vidnexa Player.
  • Huge library or TV box: Kodi or Nova.
