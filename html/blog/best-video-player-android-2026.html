What to look for in a video player (2026)

  • Wide format support — MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, HEVC and more, with no extra codecs.
  • 4K / 8K & HDR — so high-resolution files look the way they should.
  • Gesture controls — swipe for volume and brightness, double-tap to skip.
  • Subtitles — SRT, SSA, ASS and VTT, in the language you want.
  • Lightweight & fast — smooth even on mid-range phones.
  • Privacy — players that don't harvest your data.
