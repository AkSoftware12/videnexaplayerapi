How to Save WhatsApp
Status in 2025

Step-by-step guide to saving any WhatsApp photo or video status before it disappears.

📅 December 2025⏱ 3 min read✍ Vidnexa Team
💾

WhatsApp statuses disappear after just 24 hours. If you want to save a friend's video status, a funny clip, or a beautiful photo before it vanishes — Vidnexa Player's built-in Status Saver makes it incredibly easy.

No extra apps. No rooting required. No complicated steps. Here's exactly how to do it.

What You Need

  • Vidnexa Player installed (free on Google Play)
  • WhatsApp installed on the same phone
  • You must have viewed the status at least once in WhatsApp

Step-by-Step: Save WhatsApp Status with Vidnexa

Step 1 — Open WhatsApp and View the Status

Open WhatsApp, go to the Status tab, and view the status you want to save. You must view it at least once.

Step 2 — Open Vidnexa Player

Open the Vidnexa Player app on your phone. It's the one with the purple play button icon.

Step 3 — Tap "Status Saver"

On the home screen, tap the "WA Status" quick access icon, or go to the Status Saver tab from the bottom navigation.

Step 4 — Browse and Save

Vidnexa automatically shows all viewable statuses from your WhatsApp contacts — photos and videos. Tap the download icon (⬇) next to any status to save it instantly.

Step 5 — Find in Gallery

Saved statuses appear in your phone's main gallery under a "WhatsApp Status" album. You can also find them in Vidnexa's Gallery tab.

Can I Save Video Statuses Too?

Yes! Vidnexa Status Saver works for both photos AND videos. Video statuses are saved in their original quality.

How Many Statuses Can I Save?

There is no limit. You can save as many statuses as you want, as often as you want — all for free.

Why Doesn't My Friend's Status Show Up?

  • Make sure you've viewed that status in WhatsApp first
  • Check that you're connected to the internet (statuses need to load)
  • The status may have already expired (statuses disappear after 24 hours)
  • Your contact's privacy settings may restrict who can see their status

Is It Safe & Legal?

Vidnexa Status Saver works by accessing WhatsApp's temporary file storage — the same mechanism used by any file manager. Always respect other people's privacy and don't save or share statuses without permission.
