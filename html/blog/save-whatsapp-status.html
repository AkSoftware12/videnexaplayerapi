By Vidnexa Team·4 min read·Updated Aug 2026

WhatsApp statuses disappear after 24 hours. If a friend posted a photo or video you want to keep, here's how to save it to your gallery — the easy way, with no watermark.

Can you save someone's WhatsApp status?

WhatsApp doesn't have a built-in "download status" button. But when you view a status, your phone temporarily stores it. A status saver reads those already-viewed statuses so you can save the ones you like before they vanish.

Please note: Save and re-share content responsibly, and respect the other person's privacy and consent. Only save what you're comfortable the owner would be fine with.
