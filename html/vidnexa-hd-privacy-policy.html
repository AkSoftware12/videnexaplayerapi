Vidnexa HD Video Downloader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Vidnexa HD Video Downloader ("the app") helps you save videos and photos that you are allowed to download to your own phone. This page explains what happens to your data.

Data we collect

We ourselves do not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no account of its own and no analytics, and nothing you do in the app is sent to our servers. The only outside service that receives data is Google AdMob, which shows the ads in the app (see "Advertising" below).

Logging in to Instagram or Facebook

Logging in is optional and only needed for private posts and stories. The login page you see is the official Instagram or Facebook website, opened inside the app's built-in browser. Your password goes directly to Instagram or Facebook; the app never reads, stores or sends it anywhere. After login, the website's session cookies are kept only on your phone, inside the app's browser storage, so the app can open posts you are allowed to see. You can remove them any time with "Logout" in the app or by clearing the app's data.

Links and downloads

Links you paste or share to the app are used only on your phone to find the media file. Files are downloaded by Android's built-in Download Manager into the Download/VidnexaSaver folder on your phone. We never receive the links or the files.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic information about the ads you see or tap. This is handled by Google as described in Google's advertising policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's Settings (Google > Ads).

Permissions

  • Internet – to open web pages and download files.
  • Storage (Android 8 and 9 only) – to save files to the Download folder. Android 10 and newer need no storage permission.

Third-party services

When you open Instagram, Facebook or Google inside the app, those websites' own privacy policies apply to what you do on them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your responsibility

Only download content that you own or have permission to download. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram, Facebook or Meta Platforms, Inc.
